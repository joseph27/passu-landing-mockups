Learning Paths

Choose your path from the beginning.

Everyone shares the same foundation through Level 4. At Levels 5–6, your classes specialize around the French you actually need.

S

Social French

Everyday conversation, travel, relationships and culture — the French of dinner tables, road trips and new friendships.

B

Business French

Professional French for meetings, presentations, email and interviews — sound as capable in French as you are in your own language.

E

Exam Preparation

Exam strategies, timed practice and detailed feedback — walk into the official exam knowing exactly what to expect.

Paths separate at Levels 5–6 and each one runs to its own B2.2 terminus. Small cohorts may be combined.
