08

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Q.01

Do I need previous French knowledge?

No. Passu Level 1 starts from zero — greetings, sounds, first sentences. If you already have some French, a short placement conversation puts you in the right level instead of making you repeat what you know.

Q.02

Can I change from Core to Premium later?

Yes — at the start of any month, as long as a Premium seat is open in your level. Your progress, recordings, and flashcards move with you. Switching back works the same way.

Q.03

When do the paths separate?

At Passu Levels 5–6. Levels 1–4 build one shared foundation for everyone; then Social, Business, and Exam Preparation split into their own classes. When a path's cohort is small, groups may be combined.

Q.04

Is the free trial a real class?

Yes — a scheduled two-hour class with a real cohort and instructor, plus full access to explore the platform afterwards. Pas une démo — if we showed you a polished sample, you'd be choosing a program that doesn't exist.

Q.05

Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?

No — and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. Language learning depends on attendance, practice, and where you start. What we promise instead: a coherent six-level roadmap, three real classes a week, and honest exams that tell you exactly where you stand. Students who attend consistently are the ones who get there.
