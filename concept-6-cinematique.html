FAQ

Honest answers to fair questions.

Do I need previous French knowledge?

No. Passu Level 1 starts from absolute zero — sounds, greetings, first sentences. If you already know some French, a short placement conversation puts you at the right level so you never repeat what you already own.

Can I change from Core to Premium later?

Yes, at the start of any month, as long as a Premium seat is free — Premium classes are capped at 7, and we don't break that cap to make a sale. Your progress, recordings and flashcards carry over untouched.

When do the paths separate?

At Levels 5–6, roughly months nine through twelve. Levels 1–4 are shared by everyone. When a cohort is small, paths may be combined — we tell you before it happens.

Is the free trial a real class?

Yes — a scheduled two-hour class with a current cohort and a real instructor, not a demo built to impress you. You'll see an ordinary Tuesday, because that's what you'd be buying.

Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?

No — and be wary of anyone who guarantees it. What we promise is the roadmap, three live classes a week, honest feedback, and tools that tell you exactly what to do next. Students who attend consistently are the ones who arrive.
