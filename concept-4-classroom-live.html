FAQ

Honest answers, before you ask.

Including the one about guarantees — because we'd rather lose a signup than make a promise we can't keep.

Do I need previous French knowledge?

No. Passu Level 1 starts from absolute zero — greetings, sounds, first sentences. If you already have some French, we'll help you find the right level to join so you're never bored or drowning.

Can I change from Core to Premium later?

Yes, in either direction, month to month. The roadmap, teachers and materials are identical — only the group size changes — so switching never costs you progress.

When do the Learning Paths separate?

At Passu Levels 5–6. Until then, everyone follows the same core curriculum, because the foundations of Social, Business and Exam French are the same. Small cohorts may combine paths — we'll tell you before you enrol.

Is the free trial a real class?

Yes — a scheduled two-hour class with a current group at your level, not a demo or a recorded sample. You'll be treated as one more student in the room, because that's the only honest way to try us.

Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?

No — and be wary of anyone who guarantees it. Language learning depends on attendance, practice and life. What we promise instead: a roadmap that genuinely leads to B2, three real classes a week, and tools that make consistency easier. Students who attend consistently make steady, visible progress.
