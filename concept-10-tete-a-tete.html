08 La vérité · The truth

« C'est garanti ? »

Is it guaranteed?

Non.

Et c'est pour ça que vous pouvez nous croire. No. And that's exactly why you can trust us.

Do I need previous French knowledge?

No. Passu Level 1 begins at A1.1, which assumes nothing — not even « Bonjour ». The roadmap exists precisely so you never have to guess what you were supposed to already know.

Can I change from Core to Premium later?

Yes, at the start of any month. Same roadmap, same materials, same exams — you only change the size of the room. Nothing about your progress resets.

When do the paths separate?

At Passu Levels 5 and 6. Levels 1 through 4 build the same shared foundation for everyone; your chosen path then shapes the final two levels. When cohorts are small, paths may be combined.

Is the free trial a real class?

Yes — a scheduled two-hour class with current students and one of our instructors. You'll see exactly what a normal week feels like, because it is one.

Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?

No — and be wary of anyone who guarantees it. Languages don't come with warranties. What we promise instead: a coherent 12-month roadmap, three live classes a week, and honest exams twice per level that tell you exactly where you stand. Consistent attendance plus that roadmap is the strongest path to B2 we know how to build.
