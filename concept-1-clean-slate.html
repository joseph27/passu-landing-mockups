Learning Paths

Choose your path from the beginning.

Everyone shares the same foundation through Level 4. At Levels 5–6, your classes specialize around the French you actually need.

Social French

Everyday conversation, travel, relationships and culture — the French of dinner tables, road trips and new friendships.

Business French

Professional French for meetings, presentations, email and interviews — sound as capable in French as you are in your own language.

Exam Preparation

Exam strategies, timed practice and detailed feedback — walk into the official exam knowing exactly what to expect.

Paths separate at Levels 5–6. Small cohorts may be combined.

The platform

A serious program that tells you what happens next.

Passu is built so you always know what you're learning, why you're learning it, and what comes after.

Smart word references

Every word links back to the lesson that introduced it. Forgot « pourtant »? Jump to the exact moment your teacher explained it.

Integrated dictionary

Definitions, genders and real examples — without ever leaving your lesson.

Verb conjugator

AI flashcards & weekly plans

Class recordings
