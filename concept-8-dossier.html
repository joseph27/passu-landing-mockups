Annexe A · Questionsp. A
Annexe AQuestionsFrequently askedp. A

Annexe

Asked before signing, answered before signing.

A.1Do I need previous French knowledge?
No. Passu Level 1 starts from absolute zero — the alphabet, the first greetings, the essential foundations. If you already have some French, the trial class helps us place you at the right level instead of guessing.
A.2Can I change from Core to Premium later?
Yes, at any monthly boundary, in either direction. Your progress, recordings and flashcards travel with you — only the size of the classroom changes.
A.3When do the learning paths separate?
At Passu Levels 5–6. Levels 1–4 build one shared trunk; the Social, Business and Exam paths branch for the final two levels. Small cohorts may be combined — and we tell you if that happens.
A.4Is the free trial a real class?
Yes — a scheduled two-hour session with a live instructor and current students. You are deciding whether to spend a year with us; a rehearsed demo would tell you nothing worth knowing.
A.5Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?
Honestly — no, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. What we promise is the roadmap, three real classes a week, and every tool on the inventory. Students who attend consistently give themselves the strongest possible chance at B2; the work is still yours to do.
