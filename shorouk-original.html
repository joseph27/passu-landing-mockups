Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous French knowledge?
No. Passu Level 1 starts from zero and builds the essential foundations for everyday French step by step. If you already have some French, a short placement conversation helps you join the right level instead.
Can I change from Core to Premium later?
Yes. You can switch between Core and Premium at level boundaries, subject to group availability. Your progress, materials and recordings carry over with you.
When do the Social, Business and Exam paths separate?
All students follow the same shared roadmap through Levels 1–4. The specialized paths begin at Level 5 and continue through Level 6, when you join a group aligned with your goal.
Is the free trial a real class?
Yes — it is a full two-hour live class with a real teacher in a trial group of up to 10 learners, not a sales webinar. You also receive temporary access to selected platform tools, materials and homework afterwards.
Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?
No, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we promise is a clear roadmap, three live classes every week, progress exams and honest feedback; consistent attendance and practice are what carry most students to B2.
