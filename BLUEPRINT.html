The roadmap fix

This is the change Shorouk asked for, and it was a real error in concept 7 rather than a styling preference: the line split into three branches and then quietly merged back into one Level 6. That contradicts the programme — each path has its own Level 6.

The trunk ends at the interchange. Each branch keeps its own colour and runs its own Level 6 to its own terminus — Social, Business, Exam.

Two judgment calls

Her list governs layout; Série 2 governs craft.

Six of her eleven picks are concept 1 — the batch that had been rejected as not convincing. Building the list literally would have rebuilt that feel. So the structure is entirely hers, and the execution level is concept 9/6: living mesh, glass, choreographed reveals, one signature motion per section and no more.

The numbers band became platform signage, not roadside signs.

Concept 2's signposts stand on a road; concept 7's roadmap is a métro line. Back to back that is two transit metaphors in a row. The standing-sign silhouette she liked is kept exactly — pill, post, base — but the sign face is now concept 7's enamel plaque and roundel, so hero → information board → line map reads as one language.

Unchanged throughout

Every fact and price from the brief: 12 sections, $99 Core (up to 14) / $200 Premium (up to 7), 3 live 2-hour classes weekly, 2 exams per level, 90-minute optional Speaking Club, paths separating at Levels 5–6, 5% full-journey discount, four testimonials, five FAQs, four policy cards. Brand palette only, Montserrat, no emojis, SVG icons, illustrations from the design system only. Checked at desktop 1280 and mobile 393, and on the reduced-motion path.
