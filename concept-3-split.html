FAQ

Honest answers to fair questions.

Do I need previous French knowledge?
No. Passu Level 1 starts from absolute zero — sounds, greetings, first sentences. If you already have some French, a short placement conversation puts you at the right level instead of making you repeat what you know.
Can I change from Core to Premium later?
Yes, in either direction, at any monthly boundary. Both plans follow the identical roadmap, so nothing is lost in the move — your level, progress and recordings all come with you. Premium switches depend on an open seat, since those classes are capped at 7.
When do the paths separate?
Levels 1–4 are shared by every student. At Levels 5–6 you continue into your chosen path — Social, Business, or Exam Preparation. When a path cohort is small, we may combine cohorts so classes always run at full quality.
Is the free trial a real class?
Yes — a scheduled two-hour class with a real group and a real instructor, not a demo reel or a disguised sales call. You'll also get platform access to explore recordings, the dictionary and flashcards before deciding anything.
Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?
No — and we'd be careful with any school that says yes. Languages don't work on guarantees. What we promise is the structure that gets people there: a coherent six-level roadmap, three live classes a week, and honest exams at every level. Consistent attendance plus that structure is the realistic road to B2.
