## Five Character Types

**Character A — "Girl" (Polka Dot Sweater)** — most common
- White sweater with teal polka-dot pattern, pink scarf, coral pants, black hair bun
- Used in: most illustrations, exam scenes, features, marketing

**Character B — "Boy" (Teal Shirt)**
- Solid teal shirt/sweater, dark pants, rosy cheeks, short dark hair
- Used in: answer received, writing, reading, simpler scenes

**Character C — "Instructor" (Glasses + Tie)**
- Teal shirt or white shirt with dark tie, glasses, curly hair
- Used in: analytics, instructor dashboards, note-taking

**Character D — "Listener" (Yellow Sweater + Curly Hair)**
- Yellow/gold sweater, curly dark hair, headphones, glasses
- Used in: listening exercises, audio content

**Character E — "Phone Girl" (Coral Sweater + Long Hair)** — rare
- Coral/pink sweater, long straight black hair, surprised expression
- Used in: mobile notifications, reading on phone

## Illustration Style Reference

- **Art style:** Hand-drawn flat illustration, clean line-art with flat color fills
- **Line weight:** ~2px black outlines
- **Colors:** Teal `#29E4E4`, Coral `#F5A3A3`, Navy `#090E18`, Gold `#F59E0B`

## Refreshing Assets

Re-run the export script to download updated illustrations from Figma:

```bash
FIGMA_ACCESS_TOKEN=<your-token> node design-system/fetch-figma-visuals.js
```
