Questions fréquentes

Honest answers, before you ask.

The things every future student wants to know — answered the way we'd want them answered.

Do I need previous French knowledge to start?

No. Passu Level 1 starts from absolute zero — sounds, greetings, first words. If you already have some French, a short placement conversation puts you at the right stop on the road instead of Stop 1.

Can I change from Core to Premium later?

Yes, at the start of any month. Your roadmap, progress and exam history travel with you — the only thing that changes is the number of seats around you.

When do the Learning Paths separate?

Levels 1–4 are one shared road for everyone. Your chosen path — Social, Business or Exam — begins at Passu Level 5 and runs through Level 6. Small cohorts may be combined, and we'll always tell you before that affects your group.

Is the free trial a real class?

Yes — a real, scheduled two-hour class with a real group and a real teacher, plus temporary access to the platform afterwards. Nothing is staged for the trial, because the class itself is the sales pitch.

Is B2 guaranteed after 12 months?

Honestly: no — and be careful with anyone who guarantees it. Language learning depends on attendance, practice and time. What we promise is the complete roadmap, three live classes a week, and honest feedback about where you really are. Students who show up consistently walk the whole road.
